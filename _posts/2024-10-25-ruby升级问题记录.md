$ rbenv install3.1.1

$ rbenv global3.1.1$ rbenv rehash
查找 gem 路径
$ rbenv which gem/Users/用户名/.rbenv/versions/3.1.1/bin/gem

$ export PATH="$HOME/.rbenv/versions/3.1.1/bin:$PATH"
